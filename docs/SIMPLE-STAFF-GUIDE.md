# Simple staff workflow

Start with the three main pages:

1. **Main Dashboard**: choose a reporting period, review total unique participants across all ages, male seniors, minority seniors and attendance entries. The total card also shows how many are aged 60+ (or the configured senior threshold). Change **Focus** to compare the same senior audience in the trend and top-five programme charts. Use **Attendance summary** for daily/monthly records or **Print summary** for reporting.
2. **Programme Analysis**: search past programmes and sort by reach, target group or average attendance. Open a programme to review its figures and recorded session dates. Use **Plan next steps for this programme** to continue to Planning with that programme selected.
3. **Planning**: search programmes, review five suggestions at a time and choose **Record next step**. Record a decision, owner, planned action and target date. Outcome and resource notes are optional. **Saved plans** remain available across reporting periods; **Review saved plan** reopens them. Suggestions support staff judgement and do not predict future attendance.

Outreach targets are under **Outreach targets (optional)**. Programme type comparisons, extra table columns and repeat-participation detail expand only when needed. Date inputs appear when **Custom range** is selected.

## Occasional tools

Open **Staff Tools** for **Client Engagement & Outreach**, **Participant Needs & Accessibility**, **Data management** (administrators), **Dashboard Settings** and **Download reports**.

**Staff Tools → Advanced analysis** retains specialist reports and the **Detailed action tracker**, including previously saved needs-based actions. These actions and saved programme decisions remain separate records; the shared summary counts their recorded stages without exposing owners or notes. No records are automatically merged or removed.

Registered clients and people with no recorded attendance remain in **Client Engagement & Outreach**. The main attendance sheet keeps IB = existing clients, OB = non-clients, and inactive = attending people with two or fewer distinct programmes across all available delivered history. Unique totals deduplicate people.

Imports, conflict review, deletion confirmation and recovery backups remain available. This interface update needs no additional SQL migration and does not deploy the application.
